A bit more about me

Data science BA from UC Berkeley, currently a Master's student at Trinity College Dublin studying Analytics and Artificial Intelligence.

Long-term, I want to build a career at the intersection of people and data, figuring out what drives how people work, decide, and behave, and then building something useful out of it.

Outside of school and work, I'm probably planning a trip, mid-trip, or recovering from one. Traveling is one of the things I love most in life, and I'm always down to experience a new culture, flavor, or time zone.

When I'm not traveling, you'll find me hanging out with my dog, at a hot yoga class, or attempting a TikTok DIY project.

I also love trying and tracking different productivity systems, from Notion pages to color coded Google Calendars to bullet journaling. Consider it a standing people-analytics project on myself.
